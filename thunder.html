OKC Thunder

Okay so the Thunder. Seattle doesn't have an NBA team anymore since the Sonics moved to Oklahoma City and became the Thunder. So some people from Seattle hate them for that. I kind of just went with it and they ended up being really fun to watch.

They won the 2025 NBA Championship and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander was the MVP. They're super young too, so I think they could be good for a long time. You can check out their schedule and stuff on the official Thunder site.
