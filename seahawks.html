Seattle Seahawks

The Seahawks are my football team. They play at Lumen Field in Seattle, which is supposed to be one of the loudest stadiums in the NFL. The fans are called the 12s, like the 12th man on the field.

The big one is Super Bowl XLVIII when they beat the Broncos really badly. I was pretty young but that Legion of Boom defense is still kind of legendary. Sundays during the season are basically just Seahawks days for me.
