Seattle Mariners

The Mariners are my baseball team. They play at T-Mobile Park in Seattle. Being a Mariners fan is a lot of hoping and then getting let down, but it makes the good years feel way better.

2025 was one of those good years. Cal Raleigh hit 60 home runs, which is crazy for a catcher, and they made a real playoff run. I'm not totally sure they'll ever win it all while I'm alive, but I'm still watching every year.
